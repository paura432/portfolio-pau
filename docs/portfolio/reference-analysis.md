# PAU / LIVE SYSTEMS — Reference analysis

Date: 2026-09-09  
Method: review of live public portfolios. Principles only; no layout, asset, copy, or interaction is to be copied.

## Corentin Bernadou

Source: https://corentinbernadou.com/

### What works

- The work index reads like an editorial issue, not a component gallery.
- Typography, spacing, and restrained colour carry identity before motion starts.
- Technical interaction supports work presentation rather than replacing it.

### Why it works

The visual system is consistent enough that the work can vary without becoming a catalogue. Motion has a clear compositional role.

### Apply to Pau

- Use an off-white, graphite, and single-accent base.
- Treat the selected-work list as primary visual evidence.
- Make the live system a quiet structural layer, not a hero toy.

### Do not copy

- Editorial issue notation, typography, exact grid, page composition, or WebGL treatment.

## Ameen Abdullah

Source: https://ameen-abdullah.pages.dev/

### What works

- GPU work is tied to named projects and technical roles.
- The public portfolio identifies rendering technologies and interaction purpose.
- Technical depth is discoverable after the project context.

### Why it works

Graphics function as evidence of a specialist practice instead of generic decoration.

### Apply to Pau

- Keep rendering optional and profile-driven.
- Use a GPU layer only if DOM/SVG cannot deliver a real work-specific explanation.
- Pause offscreen work and preserve full textual content without the renderer.

### Do not copy

- Cinematic 3D, shader spectacle, or a creative-developer positioning Pau has not claimed.

## Ricardo Chance

Source: https://www.ricardochance.com/work

### What works

- Work is the information architecture, with direct path to contact.
- Design and implementation are presented as one practice.
- The interface removes unnecessary decisions before the visitor reaches projects.

### Why it works

The site turns a portfolio into a short route from identity to evidence to contact.

### Apply to Pau

- Use one recruiter route: identity, OVI/DUPLEX/TrustOS, experience, CV, contact.
- Let case-study interactions show product engineering rather than generic creativity.

### Do not copy

- Navigation, page order, contact form, branding, or animation language.

## Dennis Snellenberg

Source: https://dennissnellenberg.com/

### What works

- Recent work appears early and receives the dominant visual weight.
- Navigation remains sparse and the contact route is direct.
- Individual projects have visual distinction without losing index consistency.

### Why it works

The visitor reaches proof quickly, then can choose depth.

### Apply to Pau

- Put three professional cases before the archive.
- Give OVI, DUPLEX, and TrustOS distinct atmospheres within one system.
- Keep interaction secondary to title, role, employer, and timeframe.

### Do not copy

- Work-list composition, transitions, cursor effects, or visual assets.

## Emil Kowalski

Source: https://emilkowal.ski/

### What works

- The positioning is explicit: design engineer.
- Small interface details make implementation quality visible.

### Why it works

Precision is felt through responsive states and direct manipulation, not volume of effects.

### Apply to Pau

- Developer Mode must expose measured browser state, not invented telemetry.
- Use keyboard, focus, and reduced-motion states as visible craft.

### Do not copy

- Brand, components, demos, or visual language.

## Rauno Freiberg

Source: public work index: https://rauno.me/

### What works

- Product detail, interaction behaviour, and engineering explanation align.
- Interface mechanics are clear enough to inspect.

### Why it works

The implementation expresses product thinking: state changes are understandable and controlled.

### Apply to Pau

- Make DUPLEX source selection reveal a genuine visual relationship.
- Prefer native controls and explicit states over opaque effects.

### Do not copy

- Component shapes, interaction patterns, typography, or writing.

## Brittany Chiang

Source: https://brittanychiang.com/

### What works

- Role, employer, work history, projects, links, and resume are easy to find.
- Experience provides credible context before secondary experiments.
- Accessibility and concise public evidence are part of the portfolio's professional value.

### Why it works

Recruiters can assess fit before they need to understand implementation details.

### Apply to Pau

- Preserve a legible first viewport.
- Place experience, direct CV download, GitHub, LinkedIn, email, and Madrid in expected locations.
- Keep About brief and factual.

### Do not copy

- Layout, colour palette, source code, copy, or resume presentation.

## Design synthesis

1. Evidence leads; visual system supports it.
2. Static layout must pass the recruiter test before advanced rendering exists.
3. Each interaction must teach a true aspect of product or systems work.
4. Art direction changes by case study, while navigation and accessibility remain stable.
5. Direct CV and contact are product requirements, not footer afterthoughts.
